Ground rules for all labs

🔍

Never blind-confirm

IXP scores measure agreement with the labels you confirmed. A wrong confirmed value becomes the "right" answer.

1️⃣

Change one thing at a time

Evaluate after each change. Otherwise you can't tell what helped.

📝

Write down every score

Keep a simple before/after table. You'll use it in the wrap-up.
