🏁 Wrap-up & what's next

⏱ Day 2 · 1:45–2:00 · Discussion · Worksheet: below

Goal: turn today's scores and decisions into rules you will reuse on your next project.

Do now (10 minutes)

  1. Fill in the wrap-up worksheet: your rule for where an instruction goes, how you model repeating data, when you split first, and your default validation rule.
  2. Bring your score table from Labs 1–4 and share one change that moved a score and one that did not.
  3. Name one thing you will change in a current project.

Checklist – compare your answers with the best-practice checklist:

  • Taxonomy design and Instruction writing → Lab 1
  • Repeating data → Lab 2
  • Labelling & measurement → Labs 1–2
  • Multi-document files → Lab 3
  • Confidence & validation and Integration → Lab 4

What's next – topics such as performance and throughput, integration patterns (including IXP in Flow, Preview), classification with an agent, coding agents for IXP and Modern DU → IXP migration can be covered in a follow-up session. To make it about your projects, send 1–3 cases (document, current taxonomy, the issue).

💡 Key takeaways
  • A decision you wrote down is a decision you can reuse.
  • Your score table is the evidence for every design choice.
