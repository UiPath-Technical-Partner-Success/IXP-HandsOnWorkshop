Session 1 overview · Design & extraction quality

⏱ 2 × 2 hours🖥 Remote, hands-on

👋 About this page

Who this is for. Developers who already build IXP and DU automations. The Foundations page is a short refresher of the vocabulary used in the labs; skip it if you know DU well.

How to use this session. Each agenda block has a theory page followed by its hands-on lab. Follow the order in the sidebar, or the Next button at the bottom of each page: theory, then its lab, then the next theory. Read each theory page before its lab (10–15 minutes), or follow along while the trainer presents it. Each theory page ends with key takeaways, a button to the lab and the official sources.

📖 New to UiPath? Start with Foundations before the session. If you already know Document Understanding, skim the glossary and jump to Using IXP well.

Ground rules for all labs

  • Never blind-confirm. IXP scores measure agreement with the values you confirmed. If you confirm a wrong value, it becomes the "right" answer and the score goes up anyway.
  • Change one thing at a time and evaluate after each change. Otherwise you can't tell what helped.
  • Write down the score after every change (a simple table is enough). You'll need it in the wrap-up.
